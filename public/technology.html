Practical details

Setting up your UrbanLight system is straightforward and designed to fit seamlessly into your existing home. Everything works together intuitively, so you can focus on better sleep, not complex configuration.

Smart home compatibility

UrbanLight integrates with major smart home ecosystems including Apple HomeKit, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa. Control your lighting and sleep environment through voice commands or your preferred smart home app.

Setup time

Most installations take 15–20 minutes. Simply plug in your devices, download the UrbanLight app, and follow the guided setup. No special tools or technical knowledge required.

Room suitability

Our system works in bedrooms of all sizes, from compact urban apartments to spacious master suites. Sensors automatically adjust to your room dimensions and lighting conditions.

All UrbanLight devices use standard power outlets with low-voltage USB-C connections. Built-in battery backup ensures your sleep schedule continues even during brief power interruptions.

Automation controls

Create custom routines for bedtime, wake-up, and throughout the day. Schedule lighting changes, set sleep timers, and automate your environment based on your natural circadian rhythm.
